# QuantraCore Apex Gen 2 Constitution

**Status:** Task-level policy translation of `docs/GEN2_MASTER_SPEC.md`; not an
owner-signed amendment, production authorization, or F0 activation.

## Authority and precedence

This document makes the governing specification's boundaries actionable. It
does not relax, replace, or outrank the specification. The controlling order is:

1. An owner-approved constitutional amendment, limited to its stated requirements
   and effective boundary.
2. `docs/GEN2_MASTER_SPEC.md` and its explicitly approved amendments.
3. Versioned contracts and frozen epoch manifests consistent with the governing
   specification.
4. Accepted canonical task specifications and their current receipts.
5. Implementation details.

A later timestamp, implementation, task receipt, owner acknowledgment, positive
paper result, or successful development run does not gain authority by itself.
When requirements conflict, the more restrictive compatible rule applies until
the conflict is resolved through the amendment procedure in
`docs/CHANGE_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

## Mission and scope

QuantraCore Apex Gen 2 is a **private, single-owner runner-intelligence and
paper-trading system**. F0 uses daily OHLCV scientific inputs and an explicitly
selected Alpaca Paper execution account. F0 does not include live trading,
commercialization, multi-tenancy, billing, public APIs, or a required dependency
on another Lamont Labs system. No external project becomes a hidden prerequisite.

The feed entitlement, history, actual account capabilities, deployment
capabilities, owner policy, and empirical thresholds remain subject to measurement
or explicit resolution. This scope does not assert that those capabilities exist
in the current checkout.

Success is ordered by scientific integrity, predictive discrimination and
calibration, prospective learning effectiveness, frozen-strategy paper
performance, and only then eventual paid-data uplift. Positive P&L cannot cure
leakage, missing provenance, denominator manipulation, or retrospective
predictions.

## Constitutional rules

1. **Single canonical owner.** Every authoritative state domain has one
   authoritative writer, as enumerated in `docs/AUTHORITY_MODEL.md`. Owner API,
   Android, research, logs, and projections request, observe, or consume state;
   they do not acquire another authority's write permission.
2. **Evidence before claims.** Claims cannot exceed the verified data, feed,
   time, execution, and evidence class. Unknown outcomes remain unknown.
3. **Fail closed.** Missing, stale, conflicting, unmeasured, or unapproved input
   blocks the affected decision. No blank, magic value, fixture, guessed value,
   provider fallback, or implicit application default repairs it.
4. **Separate science, safety, strategy, and execution.** Broker connectivity
   cannot choose scientific eligibility or rank. Safety cannot add score.
   Execution cannot rank or trade live. Research cannot mutate production
   authority.
5. **Immutable epoch identity.** A prediction binds its complete scientific
   dependencies. Sealed evidence is not edited or replaced by a better retry.
   Scientific identity changes follow the epoch and change-class rules.
6. **Narrow owner actions.** An owner signature is necessary where specified,
   but never substitutes for a machine gate. No general override, arbitrary
   command, manual score edit, or live-order action is authorized.
7. **One canonical task at a time.** Task completion, a fixture, or a passing
   framework test does not start another task or authorize a later phase.

## F0 operating-mode rule

The F0 default is **`FROZEN_CHAMPION`**. The operating Champion and its complete
learned-dependency root, score, features, protocols, labels, universe rules, and
strategy remain fixed through the epoch. A Reference role remains bound to the
complete initial model release. Research and sealed Shadows may continue, but
eligible promotions take effect only in a later epoch.

`PRE_REGISTERED_ADAPTIVE` is an optional alternative only when an owner
explicitly selects it before activation and the epoch freezes its complete
learning, promotion, search, timing, Shadow, owner-decision, cooldown, and
compatibility policy. There is no mid-epoch switch between modes. This document
does not select adaptive mode, name a Champion, or activate either mode.

## Paper execution and safety boundary

Paper execution is secondary to scientific collection. Missing signed capital
limits keep new paper entries disabled. New-exposure pause, cancellation, and
risk-reducing exits have separate readiness requirements. A pause does not
liquidate positions; existing strategy exits may proceed only when their narrower
gates pass. An integrity, evidence, identity, or database failure may block a
new broker mutation when it cannot be recorded safely. The system must state this
limitation rather than promise an exit while offline.

No execution permission in this constitution implies a live endpoint, live
credential, live order route, or runtime feed fallback.

## Amendment and gate revalidation

Amendments and affected-gate revalidation follow
`docs/CHANGE_CLASSIFICATION.md`. An amendment identifies the exact requirements
changed, reason, impact, migration, tests, and effective boundary. Affected
acceptance gates become `NEEDS_REVALIDATION` metadata and cannot support task
advancement until revalidated. This metadata does not create a new successful
task state. Neither a documentation PASS nor this constitution is an F0
activation decision.
