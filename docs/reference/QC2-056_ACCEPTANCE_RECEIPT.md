## Verified behaviors

- A repeated active trigger increments its trigger count without producing another alert event or notification. After resolution, the same rule and dedupe key creates a new alert identity.
- Escalation does not downgrade severity. The acceptance path verified a CRITICAL alert escalating to EMERGENCY.
- Acknowledgment is idempotent and does not resolve an alert or change its readiness effect. Lifecycle timestamps and current severity remain supplemental state; the pinned `Alert.v1` contract remains unchanged.
- Automatic resolution requires the rule's exact registered condition. Manual-only emergency alerts reject automatic resolution and can be resolved through the separate manual lifecycle operation.
- Notification delivery uses the shared durable outbox. A failed push attempt becomes retryable `UNKNOWN` and visible as stale when due; it does not close the alert or change the fail-closed entry-pause state.
- Notifications are constructed from an explicit allowlist and contain source/evidence references, severity, rule, action owner, readiness effect, and lifecycle event identifiers—not secrets or approval credentials.
